02

What you’ll be able to do

  • Size the power. Split- and three-phase supply, derating, circuit sizing.
  • Run the hardware. ASIC and GPU firmware, undervolting, pool setup.
  • Do the heat math. Watts to BTU/h, COP, heating load, compute vs. heat pumps.
  • Capture the heat. Air, hydronic, immersion and hot-water heat exchangers.
  • Automate it. Thermostats, time-of-use, demand response and safety shutdown.
  • Troubleshoot it. Measure electrical, thermal and hashing performance.
  • Prove the payback. Power cost, heat offset, revenue in sats, payback period.
  • Hold your own Bitcoin. Run a node, set up a wallet and keep your BTC safe.
