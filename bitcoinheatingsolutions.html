Heat reuse · Proof of work

Buy the heat you already need. Get the hashrate free.

Hashrate heating is a form of compute heating. Like many of your tools, fixtures, appliances and home conveniences, Bitcoin miners convert electricity into heat. As with compute heating, this computing power provides revenue with clean, electric heating: it double uses the electricity you're already using and thus offsets or rebates your costs for that electricity. We specify, size, and commission these products and systems for institutions and businesses.

1 watt in → 3.412 BTU/hr out + hashrate

Electric resistance heating converts essentially all input energy to heat. Bitcoin miners function the same way, except that their ASICs amplify heat output and generate cash in form of BTC (Bitcoin). Thermodynamically the non-ASIC resistive heater and the Bitcoin heater are the same appliance — but only one of them pays you for the privilege. Bitcoin heating's advantage over other forms of compute heating is that you do not need to sell the computing power to any third party: the Bitcoin mining brings cash directly into your wallet. Everything on this page follows from that.

When can an object sit in the sun? Only light-stable materials belong in a sunlit vitrine: metals (coins included), stone, ceramics, and most glass. Paper, manuscripts, textiles, photographs, dyed or painted surfaces, and other organics fade and embrittle under light; conservation practice keeps them to roughly 50–200 lux, a small fraction of daylight, so they need an indoor placement.

Mitigating UV. Specify UV-filtering laminated glazing that blocks wavelengths below about 380 nm, since ultraviolet does the fastest damage and carries no useful light. The semi-transparent PV cells also cut visible light reaching the object.

Heat in full sun. Sunlight through the glass warms the case on its own, so in strong sun the controller should throttle the miner and let the plenum carry heat out rather than add more.
